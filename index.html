Video Studio

Video Studio is a private tool that produces videos for its owner and uploads them to YouTube channels the owner controls. Every video is labelled as AI-generated and is uploaded only after the owner has approved it.

It is not a public service: only the owner uses it, and only for their own channels.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (youtube.upload). It uses this only to upload videos the owner has approved, with their title, description and the YouTube "altered or synthetic content" disclosure set.

It does not read analytics, comments or any viewer information, and it does not edit or delete videos it did not upload.

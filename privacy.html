Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Video Studio is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

Data accessed

  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels, through the YouTube Data API (youtube.upload scope).
  • The identifiers YouTube returns for each uploaded video (for example the video id), so the owner can find the video again.

It does not access analytics, comments, subscribers or any information about viewers.

How it is used

  • Only to upload videos the owner has approved, to channels the owner controls, and to set each video's details (title, description, visibility, the "altered or synthetic content" disclosure and, where relevant, the "made for kids" setting).
  • No information about any other person is collected or kept.

Storage and sharing

  • The sign-in token and the upload records are stored on the owner's own computer.
  • They are not sold, rented or shared with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

Use of Google data

Video Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, Video Studio can no longer upload anything.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer contact shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
